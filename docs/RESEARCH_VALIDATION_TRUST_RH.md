# TRUST-RH Research Validation Contract

**Null hypothesis H0.** Trust-gating does not reduce unsafe/invalid schedule releases under stale, missing, contradictory, or incomplete twin evidence relative to an ungated recovery optimizer, or it does so only by producing unacceptable schedule instability/performance loss.

Falsification uses controlled twin-evidence degradation, a common recovery instance, gated-vs-ungated schedule objective/churn comparison, and shadow replay. Trust score is an engineering authorization score, not a calibrated probability that physical state is correct.
