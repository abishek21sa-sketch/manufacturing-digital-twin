A · DIGITAL TWIN FIRST

Residual planning starts from the current event-sourced state

TwinSnapshot → TwinPlanningState is the architectural boundary. Completed work is removed, running work is frozen/projected, the current twin timestamp becomes the scheduling clock, and machine availability carries forward.

A machine that is currently down is never silently made available: a recovery assumption is required before a residual schedule can use it.

STATE CONTRACT
St = { jobs, operations, machines, WIP, t, events }

AI, IE, optimization and future-state simulation operate on the residual state derived from St, not on raw CSV rows or a pristine benchmark reset.

B · INDUSTRIAL ENGINEERING

Factory physics, flow control, reliability and process control

LITTLE'S LAW
L = λW

WIP equals throughput rate times mean flow time. The DES independently checks trajectory closure.

KINGMAN G/G/1
Wq ≈ ρ/(1−ρ) · (Ca²+Cs²)/2 · E[S]

Approximates queue waiting under arrival/service variability for utilization ρ < 1.

AVAILABILITY
A = MTBF / (MTBF + MTTR)

Feeds effective capacity and stochastic failure/repair assumptions.

OEE
OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality

Computed only when all three real signals exist; otherwise the live endpoint returns NOT_AVAILABLE.

TAKT / JIT
Takt = Available time / Demand
Latest release = Due − Remaining touch − Buffer

Explicit release timing; no demand signal is fabricated.

PROCESS CYCLE EFFICIENCY
PCE = Value-added time / Total lead time

Lean flow measure tied to actual lead-time decomposition.

SPC · INDIVIDUALS / MR
σ̂ = MR̄ / 1.128
UCL/LCL = X̄ ± 3σ̂

Repeated observed-cycle measurements are required before live SPC is claimed.

2k DOE
Effect = 2 · mean(xterm · y)

Balanced −1/+1 main and two-way interaction contrasts quantify variability/reliability sensitivity.

TOC / DBR / CONWIP. The static/dynamic constraint drives the drum; the rope schedules release against the constraint buffer; CONWIP caps admitted shop-floor WIP inside the DES rather than merely displaying a WIP target.

D · OPERATIONS RESEARCH

Finite-capacity scheduling, robustness and stochastic policy selection

NOMINAL JOB-SHOP MILP
min αCmax + Σj wj(β + γrj)Tj

Start times, binary same-machine order variables, tardiness and makespan; precedence, release and disjunctive non-overlap constraints.

NO-REGRET SERVICE GUARD
ΣjTj ≤ TSPT baseline

AI may redistribute tardiness toward higher-risk jobs but cannot silently worsen aggregate nominal tardiness versus the explicit status quo.

BOX-ROBUST PROCESSING
p′o = po(1 + δm)

Machine-specific bounded inflation; predicted bottleneck probability can allocate stronger protection.

MULTI-OBJECTIVE FRONTIER
f = (Cmax, ΣTj, ΣrjTj)

Multiple scalarizations are independently feasibility-checked and filtered for three-dimensional nondominance.

SCENARIO-BASED CVaR POLICY MILP
min ΣpxpE[Lp] + λ[η + 1/((1−α)N) Σsus]
Σxp=1   ·   us ≥ ΣLp,sxp − η   ·   xp∈{0,1}

Monte Carlo produces common-random-number scenario losses; the MILP selects one policy. Exact enumeration of every candidate policy independently verifies the optimum.

Solver truth contract. Termination and physical feasibility are separate. A complete TIME_LIMIT incumbent may be independently validated and used as feasible evidence, but it is never represented as optimal. Gurobi is primary on Windows; HiGHS and the solver-independent validator provide independent evidence.
E · STOCHASTIC DIGITAL TWIN

Discrete events, common random numbers and tail risk

The custom DES explicitly models job release, queueing, operation start/completion, stochastic processing, machine failure, repair, preempt-resume interruption, downtime intervals and CONWIP admission.

SPT, EDD, nominal AI-risk and box-robust schedules receive the same seed set so policy differences are not confounded by different random futures.

TAIL-RISK EVIDENCE
CVaR95(Tardiness)

Policy selection uses both expected tardiness and the mean of the worst modeled tail beyond the 95% VaR threshold. Confidence intervals and P95 outcomes remain descriptive simulation evidence.

F · EXPLAINABILITY & COPILOT

LLM above the deterministic engineering stack

Gemini converts natural-language scenario intent into typed, bounded parameters and explains evidence already produced by the twin, AI, IE, optimization and simulation engines.

It does not create solver objectives, fabricate metrics, replace numerical validation or execute production actions.

HUMAN GOVERNANCE
Recommendation ≠ Execution

Every operational recommendation carries rationale, assumptions, uncertainty, modeled impact, trade-offs, evidence level and an explicit APPROVED / REJECTED / DEFERRED disposition.
